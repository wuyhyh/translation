## The MX_TIM3_Init() configures the TIM3 timer so that it runs at a frequency equal to ∼0,732Hz. The first channel is then configured to trigger the capture event (TIM_DMA_ID_CC1) at every rising edge of the input signal. The HAL_TIM_IC_MspInit() then configures the hardware part (the PA6 pin connected to the TIM3 Channel 1) and the DMA descriptor used to configure the TIM_DMA_ID_CC1
